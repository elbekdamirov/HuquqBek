HUQUQBEK 0.2 — LOCAL PLAYABLE BUILD

RUN
Install/use Node.js 24 or newer. Extract the ZIP completely, then double-click Start-HuquqBek.cmd. The application opens http://127.0.0.1:4189/.
The built application needs no npm installation. Development: npm ci, npm run build, npm start.

200 original fictional teaching exhibits and 300 case-specific decisions, plus role-responsibility questions.
Case/category/search/role selection; seven character profiles with compatible roles.
Client clarification, negotiation for appropriate civil roles, hearings, evidence read/submit/examine stages, final position, learning score, feedback, retry and JSON report export.

Environment variables GROQ_API_KEY, GEMINI_API_KEY and VOICELAB_API_KEY are also supported. Keys are not included in this package.

LIMITATIONS
Live paid-provider authentication, account quota, Uzbek speech quality and legal-feedback quality have NOT been verified. Enter replacement credentials in Ovoz va AI, then use Tekshirish. The text simulator works without providers.
All 50 cases await educator and Uzbek legal review. They are teaching adaptations of official reports, not 50 complete court-record reconstructions. Synthetic dialogue and exhibits are labelled; source outcomes and known source anomalies are kept separate.
The learning score is based on authored choices and evidence/argument preparation. It is NOT validation of a free-text legal opinion or a prediction of a real judgment. Final reasoning requires review; AI observations are provisional.
Teacher view is browser-local, without accounts, classroom sync or access control. Do not use it as a hosted institutional service without further identity/privacy work.
New-alphabet conversion is a display aid with basic substitutions; spelling exceptions and pronunciation still need review.
The LexUZ Offline installer database was studied separately; its opaque full-text corpus has NOT been decoded or imported. Cases link official legal references; historical editions require review.
Original character models remain base art. Voice animation is generic, not word-timed lip sync. Client/negotiation conversations are authored choices with optional AI feedback, not unlimited autonomous roleplay.
